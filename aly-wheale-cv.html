How I work with clients

  • Design system audit (2 to 4 weeks): Assessing an existing component library, finding the drift, and recommending structure, variants, states and documentation.

  • UX audit of a complex product (1 to 2 weeks): A structured review of search and filtering, dense tables, dashboards, bulk actions and multi-step flows, with prioritised findings.

  • AI/ML tooling interfaces: Model evaluation and comparison, classification review, human-in-the-loop workflows, communicating confidence and error.

  • Embedded design support: Longer part-time engagements alongside front-end teams. Prototypes over specifications.

  • Discovery and concept sprint (about a week): Turning a written requirement or rough idea into wireframes quickly, so teams can argue about something concrete.
